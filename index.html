SMS Opt-In Methods

Users may opt in to receive SMS messages from United Digestive through the following methods:

Website Form Opt-In

Users provide consent by entering their mobile phone number on our website intake form and selecting a dedicated SMS consent checkbox before submission.

Consent language shown on the form:

“I agree to receive SMS messages from United Digestive regarding account notifications, service updates, and customer support. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.”

Direct Website Access

Users access the opt-in form by visiting our website and submitting their information directly through the intake page.
